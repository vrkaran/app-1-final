## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, high-density enterprise operating interface for commercial and industrial solar deployment, energy arbitrage, and institutional asset management. The visual tone fuses the analytical clarity of Stripe and Linear with the sovereign reliability of modern private wealth and institutional banking suites.

### Core Tenets
- **Precision & High Density:** Prioritize structured data, tabular clarity, high-density telemetry, and rapid decision-making over ornamental flourishes.
- **Architectural Authority:** A foundational palette of deep maritime navies anchored by restrained, calibrated champagne gold accents communicates longevity, institutional-grade engineering, and high capital credibility.
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Minimalistic structure governed by razor-thin borders, subtle surface elevations, crisp typographic rhythm, and deliberate chromatic containment.

## Colors

The color palette reflects executive discipline, high contrast, and industrial durability.

- **Primary Navy (`#101C36`) & Deep Navy (`#0B1428`):** Serves as the anchor for primary command actions, high-impact headers, and key active states.
- **Secondary Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) & Light Gold (`#E4CC96`):** Reserved strictly for critical performance indicators, high-tier status badges, financial yield metrics, and focused interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Navy (`#1B2B4B`):** Dedicated to secondary buttons, interactive surface fills, and structural navigation groupings.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (`#F5F7FA`, `#FFFFFF`, `#182235`, `#64748B`):** Clean separation between working canvas (`#F5F7FA`) and operational card tiles (`#FFFFFF`), with high-contrast slate text rendering legibility down to 10px tracking.
- **Status & Telemetry (`#16865B`, `#D99527`, `#D94B4B`, `#3977C5`):** Functional tokens for power generation, battery discharge states, warning diagnostics, and system monitoring.

## Typography

Typography balances sharp numerical communication with polished executive presence.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** is designated for high-tier telemetry titles, metric summaries, and section headers. Its geometric geometry lends an architectural, forward-looking tone.
- **Inter** handles high-density data matrices, operational tables, settings views, and micro-labels. Tabular figures (`tnum`) should be enabled across all numerical outputs (grid output, wattages, financial figures, inverter frequencies).
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Titles above 28px drop in scale on mobile configurations to prevent label truncation in asset monitoring views.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses an 8px base rhythm with 4px sub-grid increments for dense telemetry rows and operational panels.

- **Dashboard Layout:** 12-column responsive fluid grid. Desktop dashboards enforce a persistent 260px collapsible command rail, a fixed high-level context header, and a multi-pane content area.
- **Data Densities:** Tables and real-time feeds support standard (40px row height) and compact (28px row height) modes for SCADA monitoring.
- **Screen Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Side navigation collapses to an icon dock.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, 8px gutters. Bottom utility bar replaces navigational sidebars.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain enterprise rigor and avoid visual noise, depth relies primarily on 1px crisp architectural borders (`#E5E9F0`) and layered tonal background surfaces, rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F5F7FA` base background.
- **Level 1 (Card/Container):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a continuous `1px solid #E5E9F0` perimeter. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Hover):** `#FFFFFF` fill with `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 20, 40, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 20, 40, 0.03)` and a border tint shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns / Flyout Panels):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 20, 40, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 20, 40, 0.04)` enclosed in a `1px solid #E5E9F0` shell.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Critical Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with a 24% `#0B1428` backdrop scrim and `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 20, 40, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a precise, professional shape language:

- **Tokens & Radius:** Standard elements (inputs, buttons, cards, segment controllers) maintain a 4px corner radius (`rounded-sm`). Medium utility items such as modals, floating drawers, and panel cards adhere to 8px (`rounded-md`).
- **Data Pills & Status Badges:** Feature a full capsule corner radius (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between actionable structural containers and informative telemetry chips.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Controls
- **Primary:** Background `#101C36`, text `#FFFFFF`, 0.5px border `#0B1428`. Hover: `#1B2B4B`. Active: `#0B1428`. Focus outline: 2px offset `#C6A15B`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#182235`, border `1px solid #E5E9F0`. Hover: `#F5F7FA` with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Gold Accent (High-Value Actions):** Background `#C6A15B`, text `#0B1428`, font weight 600. Hover: `#D4B473`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#D94B4B`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`. Hover: `#FEF2F2`.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Height: 36px (standard), 28px (dense matrix).
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E5E9F0`. Text: `#182235`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#101C36` with a `0 0 0 1px #101C36` micro-ring.

### Telemetry Badges & Chips
- Padding: 2px 8px. Height: 20px. Font: `label-sm` (uppercase, track 0.04em).
- Success (Active Generation): Background `#E8F5E9`, text `#16865B`, border `1px solid #C8E6C9`.
- Warning (Inverter Clipping): Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#D99527`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- Danger (Grid Fault): Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#D94B4B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- Solar Neutral (Offline / Idle): Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Cards & Grid Panels
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface container with `1px solid #E5E9F0` outer line. Header bands feature a sub-divided bottom border with 12px vertical padding, separating module title from panel KPIs.
